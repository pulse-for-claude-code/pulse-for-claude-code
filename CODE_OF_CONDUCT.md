# Code of Conduct

Pulse follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with newcomers, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome anywhere in the project.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or contact a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) privately. Reports are handled in confidence.
