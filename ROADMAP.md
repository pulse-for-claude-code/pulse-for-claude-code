# Roadmap

Ideas the project wants, roughly in order. Each is a good thing to pick up. Start a Discussion before building a big one.

## Next
- **Disc plays your own sessions.** Pair Pulse Disc with your own player and hear Claude Code live on your desktop.
- **Small, signed disc.** Rebuild Pulse Disc with Tauri (a few MB instead of hundreds), sign it for Windows, and add macOS and Linux builds.
- **Local recorder.** Record and play on one computer with no cloud at all: the player reads `~/.claude/pulse` directly.
- **Custom track rules.** A small settings panel for the cutter (shortest, longest, tempo) with "Reset to Auto".
- **Open data export.** Download any recording as a `.pulse` file.

## Later
- **Pulse Track Central.** A public catalogue where people share and play each other's tracks, opt-in only.
- **Sound packs.** Play any track in another style. The sound is made in the player, so a "cover" is the same track in a new pack.
- **MP3 export** of a track, for sharing anywhere.
- **"Your day" mix:** yesterday's tracks as a ready playlist.

## Not planned
- Recording prompts, code or file contents. Ever.
- Generated backing music. Pulse is the sound of the work itself.
