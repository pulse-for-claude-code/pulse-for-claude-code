## v1.0.0

The first full release of Pulse for Claude Code.

- **Player first.** The player works with no setup and opens on a starter pack of tracks, including a raw all-night session.
- **Tracks.** Sessions are cut into tracks automatically: music time at 112 BPM, pauses removed, 30 seconds to 3 minutes, every prompt on the first beat of a bar. Whole sessions stay under Raw recordings.
- **Share a track.** One link carries the whole track. It opens on any Pulse player with no account. Only the rhythm travels.
- **The puck.** Pop the player out into a small window that floats on top (Chrome, Edge and Firefox on a computer).
- **Pulse Disc (Windows, preview).** The puck with no window at all: a disc that floats on your desktop. Download the zip below, unzip it and run `Pulse Disc.exe`. Not signed yet, so Windows may ask once ("More info", then "Run anyway"). It plays the starter pack for now.
- **Recorder.** The Claude Code plugin records sessions to your own player on your own free Vercel and Postgres (Neon, Supabase or any Postgres).
