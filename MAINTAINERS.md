## Becoming a maintainer

Help out for a while: answer issues, review pull requests, ship a fix or two. Then ask in Discussions or get asked. Maintainers can merge pull requests and cut releases.

## How decisions are made

- Small changes: one maintainer's review is enough.
- Bigger changes (new features, new dependencies, anything about privacy): open a Discussion first, and two maintainers agree before merging.
- If maintainers disagree, the simplest option that keeps Pulse small and private wins.
